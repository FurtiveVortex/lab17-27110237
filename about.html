Our story

Nau Kay Baad means "after nine," a name made for the late hours.

It is a matka chai stall near campus in Lahore, under cosy lamplight.

The warm tea is for university students who study late and want friendly company.

Our clay matka chai and snacks come with buy two, get one free after 9 pm.
